### 语块（整体记忆，不拆单词）

| 语块 | 使用场景 | 原文例句摘录 |
|---|---|---|
| What do you do? | 问职业最自然的说法 | So, what do you do? |
| I work at / for / in… | 地点/公司/行业三介词 | I work at a bank. I work for HSBC. I work in finance. |
| My main responsibility is to… | 讲核心职责 | My main responsibility is to manage client accounts. |
| I'm working towards… | 讲在朝某目标努力 | I'm working towards a promotion. |
| I find my job rewarding. | 表达职业满足感 | You can also say, I find my job rewarding. |

## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| What do you do? | 问职业（比 what is your job 自然） | The most common question is, what do you do? |
| I'm a project manager. I manage teams and coordinate projects. | 报职位+一句职责 | I'm a project manager. I manage teams and coordinate projects. |
| I work for a multinational company. | 讲雇主（for+公司） | I work for a multinational technology company. |
| My main responsibilities include conducting training sessions. | 列举职责 | My main responsibilities include conducting training sessions and coaching clients. |
| This role involves coordinating with different departments. | 概述岗位内容 | This role involves coordinating with different departments and managing timelines. |
| I specialize in digital marketing. | 讲专长 | I specialize in digital marketing and campaign management. |
| One challenge is managing tight deadlines. | 委婉谈挑战 | One challenge is managing tight deadlines. |
| I enjoy solving problems and working with different teams. | 谈喜欢的部分 | I enjoy solving problems and working with different teams. |
| I find my job rewarding. | 谈职业满足感 | I find my job rewarding. |
| On a typical day, I check emails and attend meetings. | 描述日常 | On a typical day, I check emails, attend meetings, and coordinate with my team. |
| I'm working towards a promotion. | 讲当前目标 | You can also say, I'm working towards a promotion. |
| I'd like to move into a leadership role. | 讲长远目标 | I'd like to move into a leadership role and manage a communications team. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Today we're going to learn how to talk about your job in business English. | 今天我们要学如何用商务英语谈论你的工作。 |
| The most common question is, what do you do? | 最常见的问题是：你是做什么的？ |
| I'm a project manager. I manage cross-functional teams. | 我是项目经理，管理跨职能团队。 |
| Don't stop at your job title—add one sentence about what you do. | 别只报职衔——再加一句你是做什么的。 |
| I work at a bank, and I work for a financial company. | 我在一家银行上班，为一家金融公司工作。 |
| I work in finance. | 我在金融行业工作。 |
| I'm a software developer. I work for a tech company and I mostly work from home. | 我是软件开发工程师，在一家科技公司上班，大多在家办公。 |
| My main responsibility is to manage client accounts. | 我的主要职责是管理客户账户。 |
| My responsibilities include preparing reports and analyzing data. | 我的职责包括准备报告和分析数据。 |
| I analyze market trends to help clients make better investment decisions. | 我分析市场趋势，帮助客户做出更好的投资决策。 |
| I handle recruitment and employee relations. | 我负责招聘和员工关系。 |
| It's a fast-growing tech company that specializes in AI-driven solutions. | 这是一家专注于 AI 解决方案的快速成长的科技公司。 |
| One challenge is managing tight deadlines. | 一个挑战是应对紧迫的截止日期。 |
| Sometimes the workload can be heavy. | 有时工作量会比较大。 |
| I enjoy solving problems and working with different teams. | 我喜欢解决问题，也喜欢和不同团队合作。 |
| I find my job rewarding. | 我觉得我的工作很有成就感。 |
| I'm working towards a promotion. | 我正在为晋升努力。 |
| I'd like to become a senior manager. | 我想成长为一名高级经理。 |
| You don't need perfect English to talk about your job. | 谈论你的工作不需要完美的英语。 |
| Keep it simple. Be clear. And be confident. | 保持简单、清晰、自信。 |
