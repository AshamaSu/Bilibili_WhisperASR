## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you tell me about…? | 面试官开场提问 | Can you tell me about a project you worked on recently? |
| I'd like to talk about a project I worked on at my previous company… | 面试答题开场 | I'd like to talk about a project I worked on at my previous company… |
| My role was… | 亮明角色 | My role was Project Lead. |
| The management team wanted to understand the reasons behind… | 交代项目背景 | The management team wanted to understand the reasons behind the decline… |
| One of the main challenges was… | 描述主要挑战 | One of the main challenges was aligning different teams. |
| We discovered that… | 讲调研发现 | We discovered that many users were leaving the website during the checkout process. |
| I organized… where everyone could… | 描述协调行动 | I organized regular meetings where everyone could share their perspectives. |
| …improved by around… | 量化成果 | …internal response time improved by around…（from over 24 hours to around five hours） |
| I learned the importance of… | 总结收获 | I learned the importance of data-driven decision making. |
| I would involve… earlier. | 反思改进点 | Yes, I would involve the customer support team earlier. |
| What was the main goal of the project? | 追问细节（练习问答） | And what was the main goal of the project? |
| How did you handle that? | 应对追问 | How did you handle that? |
