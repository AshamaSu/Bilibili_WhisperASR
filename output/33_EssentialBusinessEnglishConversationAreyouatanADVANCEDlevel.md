## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| In light of the recent market volatility, we should reconsider our investment strategies. | 鉴于近期市场波动，我们应当重新审视投资策略。 |
| We need to capitalize on our core competencies to drive sustainable growth. | 我们要利用核心竞争力，驱动可持续增长。 |
| Developing robust contingency plans is essential to mitigate risks. | 制定稳健的应急预案，对降低风险至关重要。 |
| It is imperative that we streamline our operations. | 精简运营势在必行。 |
| The integration of AI requires careful consideration of ethical implications. | 引入 AI 需要审慎考虑伦理影响。 |
| Scalability is the ability to grow without breaking. | 可扩展性就是「扩张而不崩」的能力。 |
| I recommend we conduct a thorough market analysis before launching. | 我建议在发布前先做一次彻底的市场分析。 |
| A detailed feasibility study will help us understand the potential risks and benefits. | 详细的可行性研究能帮我们看清潜在风险与收益。 |
| Engaging with stakeholders early on will facilitate a smoother implementation. | 尽早与利益相关方沟通，能让方案落地更顺畅。 |
| We are committed to fostering an inclusive culture. | 我们致力于营造包容的文化。 |
| Address the root causes, not the symptoms. | 解决根因，而不是只治症状。 |
| True synergy means one plus one equals three. | 真正的协同效应是 1+1>2。 |
| Plan B is scrambling. Contingency is architecture. | plan B 是临时抱佛脚，应急预案是提前搭好的架构。 |
| You don't fix the earthquake; you mitigate the shaking. | 地震无法「修好」，你只能减轻震动的冲击。 |
| Customer service is a department. Customer-centric is a philosophy. | 客服是一个部门，以客户为中心是一种全公司哲学。 |
| Diversity is being invited to the party. Inclusion is being asked to dance. | 多元是被邀请参加派对，包容是被邀请跳舞。 |
| Clarity is king and precision is the crown. | 清晰为王，精准加冕。 |
| Mastering advanced business English is about using the right phrase at the right moment. | 掌握高级商务英语，是在正确的时刻用对短语。 |
