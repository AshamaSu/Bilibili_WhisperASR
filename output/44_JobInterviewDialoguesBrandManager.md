## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |

| Tell me about yourself and your branding experience. | 请介绍一下你自己和你的品牌相关经验。 |
| I started my career in marketing research. | 我的职业生涯始于市场研究。 |
| I'm passionate about building brands that truly connect with consumers. | 我热衷于打造真正能与消费者建立连接品牌。 |
| What would you say is the central part of your role? | 你认为你这个岗位最核心的部分是什么？ |
