## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Interviews are conversations, not exams. | 缓解面试紧张 | Remember, interviews are conversations, not exams. |
| Every interview is a chance to improve. | 复盘失败面试 | But every interview is a chance to improve. |
| I admire how your company focuses on customer satisfaction and innovation. | 回答「为什么想来我们公司」 | Instead, you could say something like, I admire how your company focuses on customer satisfaction and innovation. |
| I learned a lot, but now I'm looking for new challenges. | 委婉谈前任公司 | You can say, I learned a lot, but now I'm looking for new challenges. |
| That's a good question. Let me think for a moment. | 争取思考时间 | You can say, that's a good question. Let me think for a moment. |
| What does a normal day look like in this role? | 反问面试官 | You can ask, what does a normal day look like in this role? |
| Can I jump in here? | 会议礼貌插话 | You can say, I'd like to add something, or can I jump in here? |
| I see your point, but I have a slightly different perspective. | 会议礼貌反对 | For example, I see your point, but I have a slightly different perspective. |
| Could you clarify that point? | 会议请求澄清 | You could say, could you clarify that point? |
| I'll just finish this point. | 被打断时稳住发言 | Instead of stopping, you can calmly continue or say, I'll just finish this point. |
| I feel like I did everything wrong. | 表达面试受挫 | Lena said, I feel like I did everything wrong. |
| A polite apology and a smile show professionalism. | 迟到补救 | If something unexpected happens, a polite apology and a smile show professionalism. |
