# 01 · How to explain a project you worked on

> 听前 5 分钟预热：先背「核心词」，听中遇到生词查「补充词」「语块」，听完用「句式填空」自测。
> 时长 37.5 分钟 · 面试/汇报通用话题
> 提取规则：v2（2026-10-13）——标题实词硬性入选；核心词只收 B1+；语块单列。

## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Our topic today is how to… | 开场点题 | Our topic today is how to explain a project you worked on in English. |
| The project was about… | 介绍项目主题 | The project was about improving our company's customer support system. |
| My role was… / I coordinated… | 说明自己的职责 | I coordinated meetings between the IT team and the customer service team. |
| Many customers had complained that… | 描述项目起因（问题） | Many customers had complained that our response time was too slow. |
| One challenge was… | 谈遇到的困难 | One challenge was resistance to change. |
| The data showed that… | 用数据说话 | The data showed that our average response time was nearly 24 hours. |
| Their feedback helped us… | 描述协作与成果 | Their feedback helped us design a system that truly met their needs. |
| Looking back, what was the most valuable lesson from…? | 复盘提问 | Looking back, what was the most valuable lesson from the project? |
| For example, during a meeting, a performance review, or even a job interview. | 举例列举场景 | For example, during a meeting, a performance review, or even a job interview. |
| Keep practicing… | 鼓励收尾 | Keep practicing your English every day. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Our topic today is how to explain a project you worked on in English. | 我们今天的主题是如何用英语介绍你参与过的项目。 |
| Interviewers often asked me to describe a project and explain my role. | 面试官常让我描述一个项目并说明我的角色。 |
| Who worked on building the new system? | 谁参与了新系统的搭建？ |
| I coordinated meetings between the IT team and the customer service team. | 我负责协调 IT 团队和客服团队之间的会议。 |
| I worked closely with stakeholders from different departments. | 我与来自不同部门的利益相关方密切合作。 |
| We had the customer service team, the IT department, and the data analysis team. | 我们有客服团队、IT 部门和数据分析团队。 |
| One challenge was resistance to change. | 一个挑战是（员工的）变革抵触情绪。 |
| Their feedback helped us design a system that truly met their needs. | 他们的反馈帮助我们设计出真正满足需求的系统。 |
| That's a huge improvement. | 这是个巨大的改进。 |
| Many customers had complained that our response time was too slow. | 许多客户抱怨我们的响应时间太慢。 |
| We looked at response times, customer satisfaction scores, and the number of support tickets. | 我们考察了响应时间、客户满意度评分和工单数量。 |
| We launched a pilot program. | 我们启动了一个试点项目。 |
| …and propose improvements that could save time and reduce errors. | ……并提出能节省时间、减少错误的改进方案。 |
| The data showed that our average response time was nearly 24 hours. | 数据显示我们的平均响应时间接近 24 小时。 |
| Some screens were not very intuitive, so we had to redesign them. | 有些界面不太直观，所以我们不得不重新设计。 |
| The project was about improving our company's customer support system. | 这个项目是关于改进我们公司的客户支持系统。 |
| For example, during a meeting, a performance review, or even a job interview. | 例如在会议上、绩效面谈中，甚至求职面试时。 |
| Looking back, what was the most valuable lesson from the project? | 回头看，这个项目最宝贵的经验是什么？ |
| That makes sense. | 这说得通。 |
| You needed to see the entire process. | 你需要了解整个流程。 |
| Keep practicing your English every day. | 每天坚持练习英语。 |
