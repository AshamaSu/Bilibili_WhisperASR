## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's unpack this and… | 开场，表示要展开讲 | Let's unpack this and really learn how to introduce yourself in English. |
| How many times in your life have you…? | 用反问引发共鸣 | How many times in your life have you or will you need to introduce yourself? |
| If you can deliver…, you immediately… | 条件 + 直接结果 | If you can deliver a strong, clear, authentic introduction, you immediately set a positive tone. |
| It's not about X, but about Y. | 澄清「不是…而是…」 | It's not about fabricating some persona, but about intelligently highlighting the aspects of yourself that are most relevant and engaging for that specific interaction. |
| Imagine discussing… | 设想具体场景 | I mean, imagine discussing your intricate professional background, you know, complete with your degree and specific job responsibilities at a casual backyard BBQ with a new neighbor. |
| Alternatively, you can say… | 给出替代说法 | Alternatively, you can say, I'm a qualified job title. |
| This is where it gets really interesting. | 话题过渡、抛出重点 | And this is where it gets really interesting. |
| It's subtle, I know, but… | 承认微妙 + 转折强调 | It's subtle, I know, but it's a shift that can instantly convey approachability. |
| We'll explore…, delve into…, and tailor… | 课程预告（三连动） | We'll explore the foundational elements of a compelling self-introduction, delve into how to critically assess your audience, and tailor your message for diverse contexts, which is especially crucial for business English learners. |
| That makes sense. | 回应对方、表示跟上 | That makes sense. |
