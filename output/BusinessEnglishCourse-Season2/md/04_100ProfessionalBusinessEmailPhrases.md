## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I am contacting you on behalf of… concerning… | 代表他人正式开场 | Then something like, I am contacting you on behalf of Mr. Johnson concerning our upcoming partnership, adds that layer of authority. |
| I understand your concerns regarding… and would like to… | 先共情再推进 | I understand your concerns regarding the project timeline and would like to discuss possible solutions. |
| Could you kindly clarify…? | 请对方说明（比直接问更客气） | Could you kindly clarify the timeline for the implementation phase? |
| As requested, I have attached… | 按对方要求提供材料 | As requested, I have attached the report with the quarterly sales figures. |
| It's all about making it easy for others to… | 点出写作目的 | It's all about making it easy for others to say yes to your proposals. |
| It acknowledges X without needing to Y. | 说明某句话的作用 | It acknowledges a shared reality without needing to, you know, delve into specifics. |
| It significantly reduces the recipient's cognitive load because… | 解释为什么这样写 | It significantly reduces the recipient's cognitive load because it reminds them of the previous interaction. |
| And if you need a more direct follow-up on… | 提供升级版说法 | And if you need a more direct follow-up on something overdue. |
| It really underscores how powerful… can be. | 提炼结论 | It really underscores how powerful, clear, positive framing can be. |
| And to wrap up this batch, … | 收束一组例句 | And to wrap up this batch, could you kindly clarify the timeline for the implementation phase of this project? |
