## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Before we dive into the details, what is the single most important decision or insight you need us to grasp from this topic? | 逼对方先给结论 | Before we dive into the details, what is the single most important decision or insight you need us to grasp from this topic? |
| That's a great question, but we actually clarified that assumption two exchanges ago. | 阻止重复提问 | that's a great question, but we actually clarified that assumption two exchanges ago |
| We have two minutes remaining on topic two. | 报时、守时箱 | we have two minutes remaining on topic two |
| What I'm hearing is that... | 复述确认 | What I'm hearing is that we must choose option A because of the two-year longevity criteria, but the immediate risk is budget overrun. |
| Thank you, that's a very comprehensive overview of the market challenges. | 打断强势发言人前的缓冲 | Thank you, that's a very comprehensive overview of the market challenges. |
| I want to ensure we are hearing from different voices today. | 引入沉默者 | I want to ensure we are hearing from different voices today. |
| Just conscious of our time box, can you guide us to your final three action items in the next 30 seconds? | 收尾催结论 | Just conscious of our time box, can you guide us to your final three action items in the next 30 seconds? |
| Let's capture the pros and cons so we can decide faster. | 推动快速决策 | let's capture the pros and cons so we can decide faster |
| I feel concerned that our timeline will slip if we don't reassess the vendor contract immediately. | ICE 式表达担忧 | I feel concerned that our timeline will slip if we don't reassess the vendor contract immediately |
| Does this decision impact the timeline for the marketing website relaunch next month? | 主动排查连带影响 | Does this decision impact the timeline for the marketing website relaunch next month? |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The unproductive meeting. | 低效的会议。 |
| It prevents cognitive overload and it ensures focus. | 它能防止认知过载，并保证注意力集中。 |
| We have two minutes remaining on topic two. | 第二个议题还剩两分钟。 |
| I need one final point for clarity before we move to the summary. | 在进入总结之前，我还需要最后一个点来澄清。 |
| Avoid jargon. | 别说行话。 |
| Thank you, that's a very comprehensive overview of the market challenges. | 谢谢，这是对市场挑战非常全面的概述。 |
| I want to ensure we are hearing from different voices today. | 我想确保今天能听到不同的声音。 |
| What I'm hearing is that we must choose option A. | 我听到的是我们必须选 A 方案。 |
| Is that correct? | 对吗？ |
| let's capture the pros and cons so we can decide faster | 我们把利弊列出来，好更快做决定 |
| I feel concerned that our timeline will slip if we don't reassess the vendor contract immediately. | 我担心如果不马上重审供应商合同，我们的时间线会拖。 |

| Who will test the chosen solution? | 谁来验证选定的方案？ |
| By what date will we see the initial results? | 我们什么时候能看到初步结果？ |
| You define a rollback plan. | 你定一个回退方案。 |
| just ensure we're all aligned before we seek a consensus | 先说清楚大家是否一致，再去找共识 |
