## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Today, we're doing a deep dive into… | 开场点题 | Today, we're doing a deep dive into that whole dynamic world of business conversations. |
| Everything from X through to Y and even Z. | 三连铺陈范围 | Everything from like managing client expectations through to fostering better teamwork internally and even driving strategic growth. |
| Maybe you risk X, burn out the team, or do you stretch the timeline? | 两难选择 | Maybe you risk the quality, burn out the team, or do you stretch the timeline? |
| Our sources found one company facing… | 引述案例 | Our sources found one company facing like a shocking 12% drop just this quarter. |
| The main focus wasn't just… | 纠正误解 | The main focus wasn't just, hey, you overspent. |
| The strong recommendation was… | 转述建议 | The strong recommendation was, look, we need at least three more weeks. |
| It naturally flowed into the bigger picture. | 话题自然延伸 | It naturally flowed into the bigger picture. |
| Let's switch gears now to… | 换话题 | OK, let's switch gears now more to the sort of internal health of an organization, starting with something that makes a lot of people nervous. |
| They talked about X or maybe even Y. | 列举讨论点 | They talked about designated quiet zones or maybe even installing sound absorbing booths. |
| That kind of thing can really dent morale. | 说明负面影响 | Something to kind of cushion the blow and show you're still committed, you know, beyond just that initial thing you promised. |
