## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| How do you pinpoint… in a way that actually…? | 引出方法并强调效果 | How do you pinpoint your strengths in a way that actually resonates? |
| Our source defines X as… | 下定义 | Our source defines weaknesses as areas where someone might struggle. |
| Beyond just being X, what does it really tell…? | 递进追问 | But beyond just being a due date, what does it really tell an interviewer when a candidate shows they truly understand the concept of a deadline? |
| I try to stay calm under pressure. | 面试自评抗压 | I try to stay calm under pressure, and I don't give up easily. |
| I prioritize my tasks based on… | 说明工作方法 | I prioritize my tasks based on urgency and impact. |
| During my last year, I interned at… | 讲经历起点 | During my last year, I interned at a small marketing agency, and that really sparked my interest in branding and social media strategy. |
| That really sparked my interest in… | 说明兴趣来源 | That really sparked my interest in branding and social media strategy. |
| The source itself stresses… | 引述来源强调 | The source itself stresses being honest and specific. |
| This is where we see all that vocab come alive. | 收束、串联知识点 | This is where we see all that vocab and strategy come alive. |
| This is definitely where candidates can miss a big opportunity. | 提醒常见失分点 | This is definitely where candidates can miss a big opportunity. |
