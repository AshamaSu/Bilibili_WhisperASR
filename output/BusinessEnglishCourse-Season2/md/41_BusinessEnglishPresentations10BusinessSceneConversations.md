## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| And the big one, how to… | 点出重点 | And the big one, how to keep your audience engaged. |
| It's about connecting with your audience on a deeper level. | 说明目标 | It's about connecting with your audience on a deeper level. |
| They're there to support you, not distract from it. | 说明辅助材料的定位 | They're there to support you, to visually enhance what you're saying, not distract from it. |
| You don't want to be so stuck to your script that… | 提醒灵活 | You don't want to be so stuck to your script that you can't read the room. |
| Not just X, but something that makes them… | 强调更深层效果 | Not just shock value, but something that makes them think. |
| Tell people exactly what you want them to do next. | 给出指令 | Tell people exactly what you want them to do next. |
| So you've got your killer opening, you've got your visuals, but… | 三连铺垫转折 | You've got your killer opening, you've got your visuals, but you can't just wing it. |
| Let's talk engagement. | 切换话题 | Okay, let's talk engagement. |
| That's exactly what they said. | 呼应前文 | That's exactly what they said. |
| How to make sure your content flows. | 说明要点 | They talked about how to handle those with complete confidence, how to make sure your content flows naturally, like a good conversation. |

| 英文 | 中文 |

| Using visuals strategically, not just for the sake of it. | 有策略地使用视觉素材，而不是为了用而用。 |
| Designing slides that clarify, not clutter. | 设计能澄清而非堆砌信息的幻灯片。 |
| You don't want to be so stuck to your script that you can't read the room. | 别死守讲稿到看不见现场反应。 |

| It's about connecting with your audience on a deeper level. | 关键是与听众建立更深层的连接。 |
| They're there to support you, to visually enhance what you're saying. | 它们的角色是支撑你、强化你的表达。 |
| Tell people exactly what you want them to do next. | 明确告诉大家下一步要做什么。 |
