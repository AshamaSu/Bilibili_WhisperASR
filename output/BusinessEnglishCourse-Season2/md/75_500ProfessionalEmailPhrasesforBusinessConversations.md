# 75 · 500 Professional Email Phrases for Business Conversations

## 1. 词

### 核心词（听前必看，≤15 个）

| 词 | 音标 | 释义 | 原文例句摘录 |
|---|---|---|---|
| attached | /əˈtætʃt/ | adj. 随附的 | please find attached the updated sales report |
| prompt | /prɒmpt/ | adj. 及时的、迅速的 | thank you for your prompt response |
| apologize | /əˈpɒlədʒaɪz/ | v. 道歉 | I apologize for the delayed response. |
| inconvenience | /ˌɪnkənˈviːniəns/ | n. 不便 | I apologize for the inconvenience. |
| convenience | /kənˈviːniəns/ | n. 方便（at your earliest convenience） | please send the signed contract at your earliest convenience |
| confirm | /kənˈfɜːm/ | v. 确认 | could you please confirm receipt? |
| clarify | /ˈklærəfaɪ/ | v. 说明白、澄清 | just to clarify, is the final deadline Friday or Monday? |
| hesitate | /ˈhezɪteɪt/ | v. 犹豫 | please don't hesitate to contact me if you have any questions |
| inquire | /ɪnˈkwaɪə/ | v. 询问 | I'm reaching out to inquire about your latest pricing options. |
| cooperation | /kəʊˌɒpəˈreɪʃn/ | n. 配合 | thank you for your understanding and cooperation |

## 3. 词句翻译
