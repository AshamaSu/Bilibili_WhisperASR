## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Instead of saying X, which can feel a bit confrontational, you might say Y. | 委婉表达 | And instead of saying something like, your internet is bad, which can feel a bit, well, confrontational. |
| Could you check your settings? | 礼貌请求排查 | Could you check your settings? |
| Could you try checking your sound settings? | 给出具体操作 | Could you try checking your sound settings? |
| So let me try reconnecting. | 自我处理 | So let me try reconnecting. |
