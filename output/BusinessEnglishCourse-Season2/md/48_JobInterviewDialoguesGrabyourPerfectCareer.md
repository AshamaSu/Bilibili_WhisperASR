## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| No two interviews are exactly alike. | 强调差异性 | No two interviews are exactly alike. |
| How do you even approach those interviews? | 提出难题 | How do you even approach those interviews when they're so different? |
| We're going to be like interview detectives. | 设定观察角度 | We're going to be like interview detectives. |
| They're acknowledging X, which creates a positive, supportive vibe. | 说明互动的效果 | Well, they're acknowledging Sarah's hard work, her dedication, which creates a positive, supportive vibe, don't you think? |
| It's so much more impactful than just saying… | 对比效果 | It's so much more impactful than just saying like, oh, yeah, I'm a great leader. |
| You can have all the right techniques, but if you're not being genuine… | 强调真诚 | You can have all the right techniques, but if you're not being genuine. |
| Some of them feel a bit predictable. | 评价问题质量 | Some of them feel a bit predictable. |
| So our first scenario, we've got… | 引出案例 | So our first scenario, we've got Lucy. |
| Asking about X and Y. | 列举常见问题 | Asking about team dynamics and company culture. |
| We hope you enjoyed this deep dive into… | 收尾 | We hope you enjoyed this deep dive into the world of interviews. |
