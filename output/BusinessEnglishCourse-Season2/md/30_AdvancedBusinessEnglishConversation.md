## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| The sources suggest a smart way to do it. | 引述建议 | The sources suggest a smart way to do it. |
| These sources say it also clarifies… | 补充作用 | These sources say it also clarifies responsibilities. |
| Centralizing X is recommended too. | 提建议 | Centralizing asset tracking is recommended too. |
| Having employees who can X creates Y. | 说明结构性收益 | Having employees who can fill multiple roles creates a versatile workforce. |
| X ensures Y, especially during… | 说明保障作用 | Reduces downtime, especially during fluctuations or urgent deadlines. |

| Verify alignment with X, catch imbalances early. | 给动作指令 | Verify alignment with strategic objectives, catch imbalances early. |
| A core message here is encouraging… | 提炼主旨 | A core message here is encouraging a learning-oriented culture. |
