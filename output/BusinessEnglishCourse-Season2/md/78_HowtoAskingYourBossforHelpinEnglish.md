# 78 · How to Ask Your Boss for Help in English

## 1. 词

### 核心词（听前必看，≤15 个）

| 词 | 音标 | 释义 | 原文例句摘录 |
|---|---|---|---|
| moment | /ˈməʊmənt/ | n. 一会儿（Do you have a moment?） | Do you have a moment? |
| clarify | /ˈklærəfaɪ/ | v. 澄清、问清楚 | I just want to clarify something. |
| proceed | /prəˈsiːd/ | v. 往下进行 | I'm not sure how to proceed. |
| guidance | /ˈɡaɪdns/ | n. 指引（不是要答案） | I need some guidance. |
| direction | /dəˈrekʃn/ | n. 方向 | Could you point me in the right direction? |
| stuck | /stʌk/ | adj. 卡住了 | I've tried my best, but I'm still stuck. |
| overwhelmed | /ˌəʊvəˈwelmd/ | adj. 被压得喘不过气 | Another common situation is when you're overwhelmed with work. |
| advice | /ədˈvaɪs/ | n. 建议 | I'd appreciate your advice. |
| onboarding | /ˈɒnbɔːdɪŋ/ | n. 新人入职流程 | I haven't worked on onboarding documents before, so could you point me in the right direction? |
| struggling | /ˈstrʌɡlɪŋ/ | v. 吃力、撑得辛苦 | I'm struggling to manage everything. |

## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you have a moment? | 试探对方是否有空 | Do you have a moment? |
| I just want to clarify something. | 开场说明来意 | I just want to clarify something. |
| I'm not sure how to proceed. | 卡在下一步 | I'm not sure how to proceed. |
| Could you point me in the right direction? | 只要方向 | Could you point me in the right direction? |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Do you have a moment? | 你现在方便吗？ |
| Do you have a moment? I'd like to ask you something about the new project. | 你现在方便吗？我想问你点新项目的事。 |
| I just want to clarify something. | 我想确认一件事。 |
| Could you please explain the client feedback part a little more? | 客户反馈那部分能再解释一下吗？ |
| I'm having a little trouble with the reporting section. | 报表这块我有点吃力。 |
| I haven't worked on onboarding documents before, so could you point me in the right direction? | 我没做过入职材料，能不能给我指个方向？ |
| Could you help me better understand this process? | 能不能帮我更好地理解这个流程？ |
| I've been thinking about a few ideas, but I'd appreciate your advice on the best approach. | 我想了几个方案，想听听你的建议看哪个最合适。 |
| I'm making progress, but I'm having a little trouble with the reporting section. | 有进展，但报表那块有点卡。 |
| I want to make sure I understand correctly. | 我想确认我理解得对。 |
| I'm not sure how to proceed. | 我不确定接下来该怎么做。 |
| I need some guidance. | 我需要一些指导。 |
| I've tried my best, but I'm still stuck. | 我已经尽力了，但还是卡住。 |
| I made a mistake and I'd like your help fixing it. | 我犯了个错，想请你帮我补救。 |
| Asking for help the right way can actually make you look responsible and professional. | 正确地求助反而会让人觉得你负责、专业。 |
| Your tone matters. | 语气很重要。 |
