## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Have you ever totally psyched yourself out before…? | 共鸣开场 | Have you ever like totally psyched yourself out before a job interview? |
| This deep dive is for anyone who's ever felt… | 界定受众 | This deep dive is for anyone who's ever felt that pre-interview dread. |
| It's about being aware of… | 说明要点 | It's about being aware of your body language. |
| Standing tall, making eye contact, offering a firm handshake. | 动作清单 | Standing tall, making eye contact, offering a firm handshake. |
| It shows the interviewer that you've done your homework. | 说明效果 | It shows the interviewer that you've done your homework. |
| If you can demonstrate that…, it shows you're genuinely interested. | 条件 + 效果 | If you can demonstrate that you've already thought about these things, it shows you're genuinely interested and you're already thinking like a member of the team. |
| You're not afraid to own up to your mistakes. | 评价态度 | Exactly, and that you're not afraid to own up to your mistakes. |
| What exactly do you mean by X in this context? | 追问定义 | What exactly do you mean by curiosity in this context? |
| You've got the skills. | 肯定对方 | You've got the skills. |
| It shows you're genuinely interested. | 说明信号 | It shows you're genuinely interested. |
