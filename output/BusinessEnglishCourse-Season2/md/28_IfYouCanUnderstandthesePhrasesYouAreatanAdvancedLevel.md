## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| A cheat sheet of essential business phrases and what they actually mean. | 一份必备商务用语的速查表，以及它们的真实含义。 |
| Knowing exactly where bottlenecks are occurring. | 准确知道瓶颈出现在哪里。 |
| Implementing technology for real-time tracking was highlighted as crucial for visibility. | 材料强调，用技术做实时追踪对「看得见」至关重要。 |
| Implementing a formal recognition program, maybe monthly awards. | 建立正式的表彰机制，比如每月评奖。 |
| Making rewards more personalized, maybe adding exclusive benefits. | 让奖励更个性化，比如增加专属福利。 |
| The first step was identifying projects that weren't absolutely essential. | 第一步是找出并非绝对必要的项目。 |
| Absolutely critical for maintaining trust. | 对维持信任极其关键。 |
| They highlighted proactive customer communication. | 他们强调要主动与客户沟通。 |
| Stemming from communication gaps and fuzzy role definitions. | 根源在于沟通缺口和角色界定模糊。 |
| Running a business or managing a project rarely feels like a straight line. | 经营企业或管理项目，很少是一条直线。 |
| The real insight was the emphasis placed on listening to the customer after the sale. | 真正的洞察在于强调售后倾听客户。 |
| The source noted that customers were actively asking for better after-sale support. | 材料指出，客户在主动要求更好的售后支持。 |
| That is exactly the journey we are embarking on with this deep dive. | 这正是我们这次深入讲解要走的旅程。 |
| Let's see what problems kept coming up. | 我们看看哪些问题反复出现。 |
