## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Now let's move to a crucial aspect of… | 话题转场 | Now let's move to a crucial aspect of workplace interaction. |
| How do you get prompts for…? | 引出技巧 | Because how do you like prompts for a detailed opinion, a description of their experience, not just a simple yes or no answer like, do you like it? |
| When it comes to X, you'll hear… | 分类说明 | Well, when it comes to replies, you'll hear a range. |
| It signals X, but hints at Y. | 说明隐含意思 | OK, signals progress, but hints at challenges. |
| It's a nice, easy way to inquire about… | 说明句式用途 | It's a nice, easy way to inquire about a colleague's day at work, whether you're catching them in the middle of a task or just passing by their desk. |
| It asks for X without being too Y. | 强调分寸感 | It asks for general updates without being too probing. |
| What if you're specifically asking about…? | 提出对比情形 | But what if you're specifically asking about something that's currently in progress right now rather than something that's been happening up until now? |
| It implies a sense of… | 说明言外之意 | It implies a sense of ongoing action. |
| It really is, because it allows you to… | 肯定并给出理由 | It really is, because it allows you to inquire about people like teams, clients, bosses, or significant work-related things like, say, projects or even entire departments. |
| This is perfectly casual and totally acceptable. | 给口语表达「定性」 | This is perfectly casual and totally acceptable in a workplace setting. |
