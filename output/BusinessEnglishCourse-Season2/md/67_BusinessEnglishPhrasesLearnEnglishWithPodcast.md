## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome to another deep dive. | 开场 | Welcome to another deep dive. |
| Today we're going to be talking all about… | 点题 | Today we're going to be talking all about business English. |
| You could say something like… | 给例句 | OK. You know, so you could say something like I was really impressed with your company's recent campaign on whatever it was. |
| It's like you're taking the sting out of… | 说明效果 | It's like you're taking the sting out of the criticism. |
| When you start with… | 说明做法 | When you start with I appreciate your effort. |
| I still struggle with how to… | 表达难点 | But I still struggle with like how to actually deliver the feedback itself. |
| Those all work to soften the blow and keep things constructive. | 总结作用 | Those all work to kind of soften the blow and keep things constructive. |
| That's an interesting perspective, but… | 委婉反对 | That's an interesting perspective, but. |
| Tell them exactly what you appreciated. | 给建议 | Tell them exactly what you appreciated. |
| We've covered so much ground already. | 阶段性总结 | We've covered so much ground already from those essential phrases. |
