## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome back to… | 节目/会议开场 | Welcome back to the Deep Dive. |
| Our focus today is specifically… | 点明主题 | And our focus today in this deep dive is specifically leadership communication for team excellence. |
| We talk a lot about X, and that's all crucial, of course. | 先肯定再转折 | You know, we talk a lot about strategy and vision and leadership, and that's all crucial, of course. |
| It shows that we… | 说明某个做法的意义 | It shows that we trust our team and value their personal lives. |
| You start seeing symptoms, like… | 描述问题征兆 | You start seeing symptoms, like team members being hesitant to share ideas or admit mistakes. |
| You have to see it before you can address it. | 强调先识别再解决 | You have to see it before you can address it. |
| Implement changes gradually so we don't… | 说明推进方式与目的 | Implement changes gradually so we don't overwhelm anyone. |
| It's not just X, it's Y. | 递进强调 | It's not just accountability. |
| You can have the most brilliant plan, but if…, it's just noise. | 转折点出关键 | Clear goals and making sure they actually land with impact because you can have the most brilliant plan, but if the team doesn't get it or isn't excited by it, well, it's just noise, isn't it? |
| That structured approach builds… | 评价某个做法 | That structured approach, it builds momentum, instills confidence. |
