## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Are you ready to dive deep into…? | 开场 | So are you ready to dive deep into the world of customer service? |
| Today we're going to be looking at… | 说明内容 | Today we're going to be looking at some of the language that's used in call centers. |
| How do they influence…? | 提出分析问题 | And how do they, you know, influence the customer's perception of a company? |
| Those little touches can go a long way. | 说明细节价值 | Those little touches of personalization can go a long way. |
| You can make it even more impactful by… | 提供升级做法 | You can make it even more impactful, though, by adding a slight pause before help. |
| One phrase that jumps out at me is… | 引出例句 | One phrase that jumps out at me is, is there anything else I can assist you with today? |
| I hadn't even thought of that. | 表示意外收获 | I hadn't even thought of that. |
| You've really got me thinking about… | 表达启发 | You've really got me thinking about the power of language. |
| I think we've done a pretty deep dive into this topic. | 收束 | I think we've done a pretty deep dive into this topic. |
| Keep learning. | 收尾鼓励 | Keep learning. |
