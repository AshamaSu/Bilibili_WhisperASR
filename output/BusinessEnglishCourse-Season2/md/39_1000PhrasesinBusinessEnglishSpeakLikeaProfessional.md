## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's talk about X, which is absolutely paramount. | 强调关键项 | Let's talk about data security, which is absolutely paramount. |
| All those worries about X, Y, Z. | 罗列顾虑 | All those worries about data migration, user adoption, potential system glitches. |
| You've got to get the team on board early. | 给建议 | You've got to get the team on board early. |
| And let's not forget about X. | 补充要点 | And let's not forget about the integration piece. |
| That's exactly why X is paramount. | 因果强调 | That's exactly why employee training is paramount. |
| It's got to play nice with the others. | 说明兼容性 | It's got to play nice with the others. |
| I remember one conversation where… | 引述案例 | I remember one conversation where a CIO was describing a network outage. |
| Make sure your data is protected to the highest standards. | 提要求 | Make sure your data is protected to the highest standards. |
| We've got a ton of real world business conversations to pull from. | 说明素材 | We've got a ton of real world business conversations to pull from. |
| These conversations really highlight that human element. | 提炼价值 | These conversations really highlight that human element. |
