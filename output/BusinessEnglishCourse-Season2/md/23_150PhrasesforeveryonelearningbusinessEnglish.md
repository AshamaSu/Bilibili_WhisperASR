| Let's ensure everyone is on the same page. | 确保大家理解一致。 |
| This aligns with our business goals. | 这与我们的业务目标一致。 |
| We should evaluate our progress. | 我们应该评估进展。 |

| Let's avoid any misunderstandings. | 我们避免产生误解。 |
