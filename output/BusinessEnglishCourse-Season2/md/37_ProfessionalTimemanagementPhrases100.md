| Maybe even a timekeeper to ensure you only hit the essential points quickly. | 甚至设一个计时人，确保只快速覆盖要点。 |
| Another big theme was buffer time. | 另一个重要主题是缓冲时间。 |
| Maybe have short recaps during the meeting itself. | 可以在会议过程中做简短小结。 |
| Agree on the goals up front, stick to the clock. | 会前先就目标达成一致，并严格守时。 |
| You lock in the critical tasks first, the must-dos, then slot in these flex blocks. | 先锁定关键任务（必做项），再把弹性时段安排进去。 |
| It helps save that mental energy for the really critical tasks later. | 这能省下脑力留给后面真正关键的任务。 |
| Regular manager check-ins to discuss obstacles. | 主管定期沟通，讨论障碍。 |
| Ensuring the task goals are crystal clear. | 确保任务目标一清二楚。 |
