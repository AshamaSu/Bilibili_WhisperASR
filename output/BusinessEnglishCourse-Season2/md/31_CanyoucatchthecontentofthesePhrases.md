## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| These discussions really hammer home the value of… | 强调材料观点 | And these discussions, they really hammer home the value of frequent feedback sessions to fine-tune those coaching approaches. |
| A supportive leadership style fostered through X directly cultivates Y. | 说明因果链 | A supportive leadership style fostered through coaching directly cultivates higher retention rates. |
| Effective coaching fosters that crucial sense of ownership. | 点出核心机制 | Effective coaching fosters that crucial sense of ownership. |
| Let's move on then to fostering a growth mindset culture. | 议程过渡 | Let's move on then to fostering a growth mindset culture. |
| Let's shift our focus to the crucial topic of… | 转折聚焦 | Let's shift our focus to the crucial topic of grooming future leaders. |
| Cultivating X requires the ability to Y. | 说明前提 | Cultivating strategic thinking requires the ability to analyze complex trends. |
| Encouraging X can really unlock Y. | 说明激发作用 | Encouraging a sense of optimism can really unlock creative possibilities. |
| They also talk about using X to really encourage Y. | 引述做法 | They also talk about using scenario-based challenges to really encourage team collaboration and problem solving. |
| Our next critical area is developing… | 推进结构 | Our next critical area is developing robust internal support systems. |
| When employees teach their peers, it not only builds X but also fosters Y. | 双重收益 | When employees teach their peers, it not only builds their own confidence and deepens their expertise, but also fosters lasting professional bonds within the team. |
