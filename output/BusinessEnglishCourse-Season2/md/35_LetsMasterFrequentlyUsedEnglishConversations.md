## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's jump into our first conversation. | 开场 | Okay, so let's jump into our first conversation. |
| What's immediately striking is how… | 引出观察 | What's immediately striking is how the conversation just steers towards concrete actions. |
| The key takeaway from this one seems to be… | 提炼要点 | The key takeaway from this one seems to be the importance of having a structured, methodical approach to cost management. |
| The conversation then turns to… | 话题推进 | The conversation then turns to budget allocation. |
| The central challenge here is clarifying… | 点明难点 | So the central challenge here, it's clarifying that project's timeline before anyone really commits to specific deliverables. |
| They actively explore how they could… | 说明主动行为 | They actively explore how they could potentially streamline production activities. |
| They also highlight getting input from… | 补充动作 | They also highlight getting input from the department heads. |
| Providing X is also vital, just keeps things moving efficiently. | 说明必要性 | Providing quick feedback on shared documents is also vital. |
| And the fact they're even considering… | 强调积极程度 | And the fact they're even considering reaching out to HR for additional conflict resolution resources. |
| That underscores continuous improvement. | 一句话收束 | That underscores continuous improvement. |
