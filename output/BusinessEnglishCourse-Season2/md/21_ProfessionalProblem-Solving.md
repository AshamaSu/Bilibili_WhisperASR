## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I just received a complaint from one of our major clients about delays in our service delivery. | 我刚收到一位大客户对我们服务交付延迟的投诉。 |
| They will need to be vetted to ensure they meet our quality standards. | 他们需要经过审查，确保符合我们的质量标准。 |
| I believe we need a robust feedback mechanism to better understand our customers' needs. | 我认为我们需要一个可靠的反馈机制，更好地了解客户需求。 |
| I'll coordinate with HR to ensure we get comprehensive and honest input. | 我会和人力资源部协调，确保拿到全面而真实的意见。 |
| What about introducing weekly stand-up meetings to clarify priorities and address challenges? | 每周开个站会来明确优先级、解决问题，怎么样？ |
| Did we communicate this issue to the client proactively? | 我们有没有主动把这个问题告知客户？ |
| While we implement these changes, let's also gather feedback from employees. | 在推行这些变化的同时，我们也收集员工的反馈。 |
| Some employees feel their contributions aren't recognized enough, which could be affecting morale. | 有些员工觉得自己的贡献没被充分认可，这可能影响士气。 |
| Team members are often juggling too many tasks at once. | 团队成员常常同时应付太多任务。 |
| We can utilize both methods to capture a wider range of feedback. | 我们可以两种方法并用，覆盖更广的反馈。 |
| The operations team has reorganized tasks to focus on the inventory we currently have on hand. | 运营团队重排了任务，聚焦现有库存。 |
| Workshops could be effective, especially if they focus on building trust. | 工作坊可能有效，尤其聚焦建立信任的话。 |
| Ensure their concerns are addressed promptly. | 确保他们的顾虑能被及时处理。 |
| Propose measures to mitigate the impact. | 提出缓解影响的措施。 |
| Let's also analyze the current workflow. | 我们也来分析一下当前的工作流。 |
