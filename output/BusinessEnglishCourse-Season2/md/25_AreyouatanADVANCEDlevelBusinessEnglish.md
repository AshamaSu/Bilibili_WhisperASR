## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The first big theme that jumps out is just starting the conversation. | 第一个突出的主题是「如何开口」。 |
| A massive part of workplace chat is making requests, asking for help. | 职场对话很大一部分是提请求、求助。 |
| You see the direct approach like, can you send me the report by tomorrow? | 你会看到直接的方式，比如「明天能把报告发我吗？」 |
| I understand your perspective, but I see it differently. | 我理解你的视角，但我看法不同。 |
| The response isn't defensive, it's, okay, let's explore other options then. | 回应不是防御性的，而是「好，那我们也看看别的选项」。 |
| It's about being clear, respectful, and framing it as a discussion, not just criticism. | 关键是把话说清楚、保持尊重，并把它框成讨论而不是批评。 |
| Even when you decline, doing it this way builds trust. | 即使你拒绝，这样处理也能建立信任。 |
| The source shows the language is all about being clear and getting confirmation. | 材料显示这类语言的核心是讲清楚并取得确认。 |
| And specific operational stuff, too, like, can you please transfer inventory? | 还有一些具体的运营事项，比如「能请你调拨一下库存吗？」 |
| Mastering these everyday exchanges, even the simple ones, unlocks smoother interactions. | 掌握这些日常交流，哪怕是简单的，也能让互动更顺畅。 |
| Let's discuss solutions. | 我们来讨论解决方案。 |
| Let's find middle ground. | 我们找个折中办法。 |
| Even managing expectations around sharing things. | 甚至包括管理「借用/分享」这类事的预期。 |
| Navigating potentially sensitive stuff. | 处理可能敏感的事情。 |
| The source material, it's packed with examples, a whole spectrum. | 这份材料例子很多，覆盖了完整的光谱。 |
