## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Should I share the updated agenda on the screen? | 我把更新后的议程共享到屏幕吗？ |
| could you give us a quick update? | 你能简单说一下进展吗？ |
| The project is progressing steadily, but we've encountered a few unexpected delays. | 项目稳步推进，但遇到了几个意外延误。 |
| What kind of delays are we talking about? | 具体是哪方面的延误？ |
| If the problem continues, we may need to extend the timeline slightly. | 如果问题持续，我们可能得把时间线稍微延长。 |
| Please keep us updated because the client is expecting regular progress reports. | 请持续同步，客户在等定期进度报告。 |
| Our audience seems to prefer authentic and simple content | 我们的受众似乎更喜欢真实、简单的内容 |
| Website visits increased by almost 30% in just four days. | 网站访问量四天内涨了近 30%。 |
| What patterns did you notice? | 你注意到什么规律？ |
| Automation could reduce simple questions and allow employees to focus on more complicated issues. | 自动化能减少简单问题，让员工专注更复杂的事。 |
| Let's develop a proposal for both ideas and compare the costs. | 两个想法都做成方案，再比较成本。 |
| Maybe we should split the team into smaller groups. | 或许我们该把团队拆成几个小组。 |
| That way we maximize productivity without exhausting everyone. | 这样既最大化效率，又不把大家榨干。 |
| Could you walk us through the major numbers? | 你能带我们过一遍主要数字吗？ |
| That's higher than expected. | 这比预期高。 |
| This morning has already been hectic. | 今天早上已经够忙乱的了。 |
