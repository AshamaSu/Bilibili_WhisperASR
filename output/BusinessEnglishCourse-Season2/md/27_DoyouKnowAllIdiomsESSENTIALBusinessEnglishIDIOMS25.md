## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| English idioms, specifically the ones you hear all the time in business. | 英语习语，尤其是商务场合天天听到的那些。 |
| Our mission is to unpack these idioms, look at their meaning. | 我们的任务是把这些习语拆开讲清楚它们的含义。 |
| Even fluent speakers can struggle. | 就连说得很流利的人也会卡壳。 |
| Waiting for approval becomes waiting for the green light. | 「等审批」被说成「等绿灯」。 |
| It means you're maybe behind competitors, behind schedule, behind on your workload. | 它的意思是你可能落后于对手、进度落后、工作量也堆积。 |
| Results, decisions, the nitty-gritty outcomes. | 结果、决策，那些最实在的成果。 |
| Yeah, exactly like a traffic signal. | 对，就像交通信号灯一样。 |
| Mastering these is key to sounding natural. | 掌握这些是说得自然的关键。 |
| Even with the green light, sometimes things are fuzzy. | 就算拿到了绿灯，有时事情仍然模糊。 |
| Simple enough, it just means to start something, get a process or project going. | 很简单，就是「启动某事」，把流程或项目跑起来。 |
| What about when you do address something perfectly? | 那当你把某件事处理得恰到好处时呢？ |
| Our source had examples like, let's schedule a meeting to get the ball rolling. | 材料里的例子比如「我们约个会先把事情启动起来」。 |
| Everyone talks about the bottom line. | 人人都谈「底线/最终结果」。 |
| Today we're tackling something that often trips people up. | 今天我们讲一个经常让人栽跟头的东西。 |
| Making sure those little details are perfect. | 确保那些小细节都完美。 |
