## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's start with this conversation labeled… | 引出案例 | Let's start with this conversation labeled the Wilson Project. |
| This conversation is like a master class in… | 高度评价 | So this conversation is like a master class in subtle negotiation tactics. |
| Speaking of X, let's shift gears and move on to… | 转场 | Speaking of tricky situations, let's shift gears and move on to this contract negotiation. |
| They're talking about upgrading X, implementing Y. | 罗列措施 | They're talking about upgrading their security infrastructure, implementing regular penetration testing. |
| We're talking about X, Y, and the need to make sure… | 三连铺陈 | We're talking about regulatory hurdles, cultural nuances, and the need to make sure that your product or service is actually appealing to local tastes. |
| This conversation really highlights the complexities of… | 提炼主题 | This conversation really highlights the complexities of going global. |
| Now let's shift our focus to… | 结构过渡 | Now let's shift our focus to the global stage. |
| We're getting into the nitty gritty of how they're going to… | 深入细节 | We're getting into the nitty gritty of how they're going to make it happen. |
| They're also keeping the client informed every step of the way. | 描述做法 | They're also keeping the client informed every step of the way. |
| If you could eavesdrop on…, you'd hear… | 设想场景 | If you could eavesdrop on a global water cooler chat. |
