## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| That explains why you look exhausted already. | 顺理成章地回应 | That explains why you look exhausted already. |
| Thanks for catching it. | 感谢对方发现问题 | Thanks for catching it. |
| I accidentally sent the wrong spreadsheet to the finance department yesterday. | 坦白具体失误 | I accidentally sent the wrong spreadsheet to the finance department yesterday. |
| I already sent the corrected version along with an apology email. | 说明补救动作 | I already sent the corrected version along with an apology email. |
| We are currently 85% complete. | 给量化进度 | We are currently 85% complete. |
| Sorry to interrupt, but could you explain what customer engagement means in simple terms? | 打断并请解释术语 | Sorry to interrupt, but could you explain what customer engagement means in simple terms? |

| Let's go through it together before the meeting. | 约一起过一遍 | Let's go through it together before the meeting. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| How was your commute today? | 今天通勤怎么样？ |
| The train was delayed for almost 20 minutes. | 地铁晚了将近 20 分钟。 |
| That explains why you look exhausted already. | 难怪你看起来已经累趴了。 |
| I accidentally sent the wrong spreadsheet to the finance department yesterday. | 我昨天不小心把错的表格发给了财务部。 |
| I already sent the corrected version along with an apology email. | 我已经把更正版连同一封道歉邮件一起发了。 |
| People care more about how you fix mistakes than the mistakes themselves. | 人们更在意你怎么补救，而不是错误本身。 |
| I definitely learned to double-check email recipients after that. | 那之后我绝对学会了发邮件前再核对收件人。 |
| We are currently 85% complete. | 我们目前完成 85%。 |
| When someone says facing an issue, it sounds more professional than having a problem, right? | 说「遇到一个问题」比说「有个麻烦」听起来专业，对吧？ |
| That will require optimization from the technical team. | 那需要技术团队做优化。 |
| Yes, we will prioritize that this week. | 是的，我们这周优先处理。 |
| Before we start, I'd like to confirm, are we presenting the full version or just the prototype? | 开始前我确认一下，我们讲完整版还是只讲原型？ |
| Sorry to interrupt, but could you explain what customer engagement means in simple terms? | 抱歉打断，能否用简单的话解释一下客户互动指什么？ |
| That's a valid point. | 这个意见站得住脚。 |
| I think the design is strong, but we may need to simplify the navigation menu. | 我觉得设计很好，但导航菜单可能要简化。 |
| Thanks for your patience. | 感谢你的耐心。 |
