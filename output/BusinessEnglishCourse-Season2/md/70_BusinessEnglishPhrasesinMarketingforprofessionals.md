## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |

| It's about creating a unified customer journey. | 给出新目标 | It's about creating a unified customer journey. |
| It's not just a marketing gimmick. | 强调不是做样子 | It's not just a marketing gimmick. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| We're diving into the world of sales and marketing through a unique lens. | 我们用一个独特的视角切入销售与营销这个世界。 |
| They're two sides of the same coin. | 它们是同一枚硬币的两面。 |
| You know, silos between departments are a recipe for disaster. | 你知道，部门之间各自为政就是灾难的配方。 |
| The companies that are truly thriving are the ones where sales and marketing are seamlessly integrated | 真正活得好的公司，是销售与营销无缝打通的那些。 |
| It's all about finding that balance between delivering personalized experiences and respecting user privacy. | 关键就是在提供个性化体验与尊重用户隐私之间找到平衡。 |
| they're shifting towards what's called contextual targeting | 他们正在转向所谓的按语境投放。 |
| It's about creating a unified customer journey. | 关键是要打造统一的客户旅程。 |
| Greenwashing won't cut it. | 漂绿那一套行不通。 |
| Consumers are savvy and they can spot inauthenticity from a mile away. | 消费者很精明，装模作样隔着老远就能看出来。 |
| They're walking the walk, not just talking the talk. | 他们是说到做到，而不只是嘴上说说。 |
| She sees immersive technologies like augmented reality AR and virtual reality VR becoming major players | 她认为增强现实和虚拟现实这类沉浸式技术将成为重要角色。 |
| Learn to delegate tasks whenever possible and ask for help when you need it. | 尽可能学会分派任务，需要时就开口求助。 |
| It's a reminder that success isn't just about climbing the corporate ladder. | 这提醒我们，成功不只是往上爬。 |
| So it's about being a problem solver, not just a complainer. | 所以关键在于当解决问题的人，而不只是抱怨的人。 |
| What works for one person might not work for another. | 适合一个人的办法未必适合另一个人。 |
| It's about being intentional with your time and energy rather than letting work consume every aspect of your life. | 关键是有意识地安排你的时间和精力，而不是让工作吞掉你生活的每个角落。 |
