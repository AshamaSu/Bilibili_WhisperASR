## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome to our deep dive into X, specifically for Y. | 开场定位 | Welcome to our deep dive into business English, specifically for finance. |
| It's time to jump into a company's financials. | 进入主题 | So I guess that means it's time to jump into a company's financials. |
| If one X takes a hit, you've got others to keep things going. | 说明分散风险 | If one revenue stream takes a hit, you've got others to keep things going. |
| Another term you'll hear a lot is… | 引出术语 | Another term you'll hear a lot is market capitalization. |
| We've got some really interesting stuff here on X. | 预告内容 | We've got some really interesting stuff here on revenue streams and profit margins. |
| We've covered a lot of ground, from X to Y. | 总结覆盖范围 | We've covered a lot of ground, from the basics to some pretty advanced stuff. |
| One of our sources mentions X, says it's important to… | 引述材料 | One of our sources mentions cash flow. |
| I'm ready to be a financial detective. | 自嘲式表态 | I'm ready to be a financial detective, I guess. |
| That makes sense. | 表示理解 | That makes sense. |
| A company's got multiple ways to make money. | 陈述事实 | If a company's got multiple ways to make money. |
