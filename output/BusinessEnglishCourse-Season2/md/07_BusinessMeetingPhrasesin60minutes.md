## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| We're taking a really deep dive into… | 开场点题 | We're taking a really deep dive into essential business English phrases and acronyms. |
| X stands for… | 解释缩略语 | First one, EOD stands for end of day. |
| X means…, saves typing, makes… crystal clear. | 说明用法收益 | EOD means by the close of business on that day, saves typing, makes the deadline crystal clear instantly. |
| Keeping people in the loop means… | 下定义式解释 | Keeping people in the loop means making sure all the relevant people know what's going on. |
| This could be a noun… or a verb… | 说明词性两用 | And this could be a noun, let's have a debrief, or a verb, we need a debrief. |
| A scalable business can reach… without… | 解释形容词的实际含义 | A scalable business can reach a much bigger market, maybe globally, without its costs exploding. |
| Do you have a minute to hop on a call? | 临时约通话 | Do you have a minute to hop on a call? |
| Just wanted to circle back to… | 重新提起旧话题 | Just wanted to circle back to my question from last week. |
| We need to focus on… | 收束聚焦 | We need to focus on the main client deliverable. |
| Get ready for some of those aha moments. | 预告收获 | Get ready for some of those aha moments. |
