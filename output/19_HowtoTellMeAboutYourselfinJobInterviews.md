## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself. | 请介绍一下你自己。 |
| This question appears in many situations: a job interview, a networking event, or a meeting. | 这个问题出现在很多场合：面试、社交活动或会议。 |
| I usually start with a brief introduction about my professional background. | 我通常会先简要介绍我的职业背景。 |
| My name is David and I'm a marketing professional with several years of experience. | 我叫 David，是一名有多年经验的营销从业者。 |
| I studied business administration at university. | 我在大学学的是工商管理。 |
| One of my strengths is my ability to analyze market trends. | 我的优势之一是分析市场趋势的能力。 |
| I helped launch a social media campaign that increased engagement by more than 30%. | 我参与发起的社交媒体活动让互动量提升了 30% 以上。 |
| The goal is to provide useful information without making the introduction too long. | 目标是提供有用信息，同时不让介绍太长。 |
| It's usually better to keep the introduction focused and relevant. | 介绍最好保持聚焦、切题。 |
| Currently, I'm looking for opportunities where I can continue developing my skills. | 目前我在寻找能继续提升技能的机会。 |
| Outside of work, I enjoy reading, traveling, and learning new languages. | 工作之余，我喜欢阅读、旅行和学新语言。 |
| Hello everyone, my name is David. I recently joined the marketing team. | 大家好，我叫 David，刚加入市场团队。 |
| I'm really excited to collaborate with all of you. | 我非常期待与各位合作。 |
| In interviews, a good answer focuses on three things: background, experience, and goals. | 面试中好的回答聚焦三点：背景、经验和目标。 |
| Ideally, your introduction should connect your past, your present, and your future. | 理想的自我介绍应该把过去、现在和未来串起来。 |
| Don't memorize it word for word—it might sound robotic. | 别一字一句背稿——那样会显得机械。 |
| Communication is more important than perfection. | 沟通比完美更重要。 |
| Most people appreciate effort and clarity more than perfect grammar. | 大多数人在意的是努力和清晰，而不是完美语法。 |
| Your self-introduction does not need to be perfect. It needs to be clear and sincere. | 自我介绍不必完美，但要清晰、真诚。 |
| Confidence grows with practice. | 自信在练习中生长。 |
