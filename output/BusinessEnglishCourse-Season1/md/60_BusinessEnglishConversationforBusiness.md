## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I wanted to discuss the upcoming quarterly review meeting. | 我想聊聊接下来那次季度复盘会。 |
| Let's move on to the budget review. | 我们接着看预算。 |
| I also want to touch upon marketing strategies for the next quarter. | 我还想提一下下季度的营销策略。 |
| Our social media campaigns performed better than expected. | 我们的社交媒体推广效果比预期好。 |
| Regarding client feedback, there are recurring concerns about response times. | 关于客户反馈，响应速度是反复被提到的问题。 |
| Could you prepare a proposal with costs and timelines by the end of the week? | 你能否在周末前准备一份带成本和时间表的提案？ |
| Can you circulate a summary of the new timeline to all stakeholders? | 你能否把新时间表的摘要发给所有相关方？ |
| I'll draft it and share it with you for approval. | 我先起草，然后发给你审批。 |
| I'll coordinate with logistics to address them promptly. | 我会和物流那边协调，尽快处理。 |
| Implementing a new CRM system could streamline communication. | 上一套新的 CRM 系统可以把沟通打通。 |
| Do we need to adjust the budget allocation next quarter? | 下季度我们需要调整预算分配吗？ |
| Offering hybrid options improves productivity and job satisfaction. | 提供混合办公选项能提升效率和满意度。 |
| Should we continue with the same approach or introduce new content to reach a broader audience? | 我们该沿用原来的做法，还是引入新内容触达更广的人群？ |
| Keep what's successful and gradually explore new demographics. | 保留有效的部分，逐步探索新客群。 |
| The revised timeline ensures no compromise on quality. | 修订后的时间表保证质量不打折。 |
| Personalized presentations will help us stand out. | 个性化的演示能让我们脱颖而出。 |
