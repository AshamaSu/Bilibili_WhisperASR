## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Okay, so your goal, your professional ambition isn't just to sit in on those big, important business meetings. | 你的目标、你的职业野心，不只是坐在那些重要会议的现场。 |
| We're basically looking at the anatomy of corporate conversation. | 我们其实是在拆解公司对话的构造。 |
| Let's start by really examining the language professionals use when they're launching, developing, executing major initiatives. | 我们先仔细看看专业人士在启动、推进、执行重大项目时用的语言。 |
| What they really reveal is that genuine professional dialogue, especially at higher levels, it's highly standardized. | 它们真正揭示的是：真正的职业对话，尤其在高层面，其实高度标准化。 |
| The required metrics are explicitly stated. | 要求的指标会被明确说出来。 |
| Furthermore, success isn't optional, and it's measured in hard data right from that first kickoff meeting. | 而且成功不是可选项，从第一次启动会起就用硬数据衡量。 |
| The standard expected response has to reference a thorough risk assessment having been done and the inclusion of specific contingency plans. | 标准答案必须提到已做过完整风险评估，并有具体的应急预案。 |
| So it prevents things like scope creep or fuzzy goals. | 这样就能防止范围蔓延或目标含糊。 |
| And crucially, what's the specific 12 week rollout period? | 关键是，这 12 周的推广期具体怎么安排？ |
| They flag things like cultural alignment and tech stack compatibility as immediate high priority hurdles. | 他们指出文化契合度、技术栈兼容性等是最优先要跨的坎。 |
| Mentioning it shows you're serious about CX and using benchmarkable data. | 提到它，就说明你重视客户体验、在用可对标的数据。 |
| Over the planned 12-month transformation period, success is measured using hard data, employee engagement scores, and productivity indicators. | 在计划的 12 个月转型期内，用硬数据、员工敬业度得分和生产率指标来衡量成功。 |
| There's absolutely no room for speculation or blame at this stage. | 这个阶段完全没有猜测或追责的空间。 |
| Things like what are the key four objectives? | 比如：关键的四项目标是什么？ |
| Here it seems the whole conversation is built around justifying why this product should even exist, its market viability, how it beats competitors. | 这里整段对话似乎都在论证这产品为什么该存在、市场可行性如何、凭什么赢过对手。 |
| You're showing you're being careful with the company's investment. | 这表示你在替公司的投入把关。 |
