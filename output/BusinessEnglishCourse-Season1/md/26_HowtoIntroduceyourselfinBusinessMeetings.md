## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Good morning, everyone. | 大家早上好。 |
| I had just joined a new company and on my first day I had to introduce myself to the whole team. | 我刚入职一家新公司，第一天就得向全团队做自我介绍。 |
| Start with a greeting, then your name. | 先问候，再说名字。 |
| Greeting plus name plus position plus company plus key responsibility plus purpose. | 问候＋姓名＋职位＋公司＋主要职责＋目的。 |
| Second, your job title or role. | 第二，说你的头衔或角色。 |
| I'm responsible for overseeing cross-border logistics. | 我负责监管跨境物流。 |
| Because clients need to know exactly who is responsible for what. | 因为客户需要清楚谁负责什么。 |
| For example, I oversee client accounts in the European market. | 比如，我负责欧洲市场的客户账户。 |
| My background is logistics and workflow planning. | 我的背景是物流和流程规划。 |
| I look forward to collaborating with you. | 期待与各位合作。 |
| It's a pleasure to meet you and we appreciate the opportunity to work together. | 很高兴认识你，感谢有机会合作。 |
| Ending with enthusiasm creates a friendly tone. | 用热情收尾能营造友好的语气。 |
| Maintain eye contact, smile naturally, sit or stand upright, and speak clearly. | 保持眼神交流、自然微笑、坐姿站姿端正、吐字清晰。 |
| Yes, a natural smile shows confidence and approachability. | 对，自然的微笑体现自信和亲和力。 |
| Maintain eye contact, sit upright, and offer a firm handshake if appropriate. | 保持眼神交流、坐直，场合合适时有力地握个手。 |
| If your name is often mispronounced, you can offer a nickname. | 如果你的名字常被读错，可以主动给个好叫的昵称。 |
| There may be audio delays or background noise. | 可能有声音延迟或背景噪音。 |
| Joining a meeting as a new employee. | 以新员工身份参加会议。 |
