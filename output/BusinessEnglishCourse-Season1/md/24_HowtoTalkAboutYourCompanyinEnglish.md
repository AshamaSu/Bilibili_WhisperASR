## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I work for a mid-sized technology company that specializes in… | 一句话介绍公司 | I work for a mid-sized technology company that specializes in cloud-based solutions. |
| Our mission is to… | 讲公司使命 | Our mission is to make financial services more accessible and affordable. |
| The company was founded in… and has grown steadily. | 讲公司历史 | The company was founded in 2008 and has grown steadily over the past decade. |
| Our headquarters are based in… | 讲总部位置 | Our headquarters are based in Amsterdam. |
| We're headquartered in… | 同上（动词用法） | Or we're headquartered in London. |
| We operate in… sector. | 讲所在行业 | We operate in the renewable energy sector. |
| We primarily serve clients in… | 讲主要客户群 | We primarily serve clients in the financial and telecommunications sectors. |
| We provide… for… | 讲产品服务对象 | We provide software solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. |
| We started as a small startup and have since expanded into… | 讲发展历程 | We started as a small startup and have since expanded into multiple international markets. |
| We achieved a 15% increase in revenue and expanded our market share. | 讲业绩数据 | We achieved a 15% increase in revenue and expanded our market share in the Asia-Pacific region. |
| First, you should identify the company and the industry. | 讲介绍顺序 | First, you should identify the company and the industry. |
| Other useful adjectives include innovative, market-leading, data-driven, client-focused. | 给描述形容词 | Other useful adjectives include innovative, market-leading, data-driven, client-focused. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Today we're going to talk about how to talk about your company in English. | 今天我们聊如何用英语介绍自己的公司。 |
| I work for a mid-sized technology company that specializes in cloud-based solutions. | 我在一家专攻云解决方案的中型科技公司工作。 |
| Our mission is to make financial services more accessible and affordable. | 我们的使命是让金融服务更易得、更实惠。 |
| The company was founded in 2008 and has grown steadily over the past decade. | 公司成立于 2008 年，过去十年稳步增长。 |
| Our headquarters are based in Amsterdam. | 我们的总部在阿姆斯特丹。 |
| Or we're headquartered in London. | 或者说：我们总部设在伦敦。 |
| We operate in the renewable energy sector. | 我们从事可再生能源行业。 |
| We primarily serve clients in the financial and telecommunications sectors. | 我们的主要客户来自金融和电信行业。 |
| We provide software solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. | 我们为中小型企业提供软件解决方案。 |
| We started as a small startup and have since expanded into multiple international markets. | 我们从一家小初创公司起步，如今已扩展到多个国际市场。 |
| We achieved a 15% increase in revenue and expanded our market share in the Asia-Pacific region. | 我们营收增长了 15%，并扩大了在亚太地区的市场份额。 |
| First, you should identify the company and the industry. | 首先，要点明公司和所在行业。 |
| It helps the listener understand whether your company is a startup, a national firm, or a global player. | 这能帮听众判断你的公司是初创、国内公司还是全球化企业。 |
| Let's talk about company mission and values. | 我们来聊聊公司的使命和价值观。 |
| Let's talk about competitive advantage. | 我们来聊聊竞争优势。 |
| Company culture describes the values, environment. | 公司文化描述的是价值观和环境。 |
| Other useful adjectives include innovative, market-leading, data-driven, client-focused. | 其他好用的形容词还有：创新的、市场领先的、数据驱动的、以客户为中心的。 |
| You can mention where your headquarters are. | 你可以提一下总部在哪里。 |
