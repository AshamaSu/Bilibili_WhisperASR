## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Let's start with the base case. | 我们从基准情形开始。 |
| With an initial investment of $4.5 million, the projected NPV stands at a positive $820,000. | 初始投资 450 万美元，预计净现值为正的 82 万美元。 |
| What does the NPV look like at our hurdle rate of 12%? | 按我们 12% 的门槛收益率，净现值是多少？ |
| An 18.5% IRR is certainly promising, especially with a 12% hurdle rate. | 18.5% 的内部收益率确实不错，特别是有 12% 的门槛。 |
| How does that align with our payback period target? | 这和我们的回收期目标吻合吗？ |
| The base case NPV drops from $820,000 to $450,000. | 基准情形的净现值从 82 万降到 45 万。 |
| What is the impact of removing the launch delay? | 去掉上市延迟这一项，影响有多大？ |
| That's a classic translation risk for an export business. | 对出口业务来说，这是典型的折算风险。 |
| I propose we secure six-month forward contracts. | 我建议锁定六个月远期合约。 |
| Trade credit insurance transfers the credit risk off our balance sheet. | 应收保险把信用风险移出资产负债表。 |
| We need to implement risk mitigation strategies before confirming those contracts. | 确认合同前，我们必须先落实风险缓解措施。 |
| Now let's pivot to the second major issue, credit risk. | 现在转到第二个主要问题：信用风险。 |
| It protects our balance sheet from a catastrophic client default. | 它保护资产负债表免受客户重大违约冲击。 |
| These measures would reduce year 2 operating costs by an estimated 10%. | 这些措施预计能把第二年运营成本降低 10%。 |
| The standard payback period is within four years. | 标准回收期是四年以内。 |
