## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue increased by 12% compared to the previous quarter. | 报环比增长 | Revenue increased by 12% compared to the previous quarter. |
| Sales increased by 8% year over year while operating expenses decreased by 2%. | 同比+对照 | This quarter, sales increased by 8% year over year while operating expenses decreased by 2%. |
| We achieved 95% of our quarterly sales target. | 报目标完成率 | We achieved 95% of our quarterly sales target. |
| Overall, the company delivered solid growth this quarter. | 先给总结 | Overall, the company delivered solid growth this quarter. |
| We exceeded our targets. | 报超额完成 | We exceeded our targets. |
| Revenue declined by 6%, primarily due to… | 报下滑+原因 | This quarter revenue declined by 6% compared to the previous quarter, primarily due to… |
| While sales volume increased, profit margins decreased due to higher production costs. | 转折对照 | While sales volume increased, profit margins decreased due to higher production costs. |
| Profit margins improved due to cost management initiatives. | 讲改善动因 | Profit margins improved due to cost management initiatives and supply chain efficiencies. |
| Based on current trends, we anticipate moderate revenue growth next quarter. | 给预测 | Based on current trends, we anticipate moderate revenue growth next quarter. |
| We forecast / we are targeting… | 预测措辞 | We expect, we forecast, we are targeting. |
| How do you describe a decline professionally? | 问下降表达 | How do you describe a decline professionally? |
| Improved and lower are not precise enough. Add numbers and context. | 提醒要具体 | Improved and lower are not precise enough. Add numbers and context. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| How to talk about business performance in English. | 如何用英语谈业务表现。 |
| Revenue increased by 12% compared to the previous quarter. | 营收环比增长了 12%。 |
| Sales increased by 8% year over year while operating expenses decreased by 2%. | 销售额同比增长 8%，同时运营开支下降 2%。 |
| We achieved 95% of our quarterly sales target. | 我们完成了季度销售目标的 95%。 |
| Overall, the company delivered solid growth this quarter. | 总体来看，公司本季度实现了稳健增长。 |
| We exceeded our targets. | 我们超额完成了目标。 |
| This quarter revenue declined by 6% compared to the previous quarter. | 本季度营收环比下降了 6%。 |
| While sales volume increased, profit margins decreased due to higher production costs. | 虽然销量上升，但因生产成本上涨，利润率下降了。 |
| Profit margins improved due to cost management initiatives and supply chain efficiencies. | 得益于成本管理举措和供应链效率提升，利润率有所改善。 |
| Based on current trends, we anticipate moderate revenue growth next quarter. | 基于当前趋势，我们预计下季度营收将温和增长。 |
| We expect, we forecast, we are targeting. | 我们预计、我们预测、我们的目标是——三种预测措辞。 |
| Forecasts should sound data-driven, not emotional. | 预测要显得有数据支撑，而不是情绪化。 |
| Improved and lower are not precise enough. Add numbers and context. | 「有改善」「更低」不够精确，要加上数字和背景。 |
| KPI stands for Key Performance Indicator. | KPI 的全称是关键绩效指标。 |
| Sales teams focus on revenue and targets. | 销售团队关注营收和目标。 |
| Executives may care about profitability. Operations may care about efficiency. | 高管关心盈利能力，运营部门关心效率。 |
| Comparisons are essential. | 对比表述必不可少。 |
| How do you describe a decline professionally? | 怎样专业地描述业绩下滑？ |
