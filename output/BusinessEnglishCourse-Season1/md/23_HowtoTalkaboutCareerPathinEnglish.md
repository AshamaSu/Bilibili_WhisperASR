## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I started my career in… as… | 讲职业起点 | Certainly, I started my career in operations as a logistics coordinator. |
| After two years, I was promoted to… | 讲晋升 | After two years, I was promoted to marketing executive, which allowed me to manage client relationships. |
| Eventually, I transitioned into… | 讲转型 | Eventually, I transitioned into digital marketing, specializing in data analytics. |
| After five years in…, I decided to pivot into… | 讲转行 | After five years in finance, I decided to pivot into the technology sector. |
| A career path includes promotions, industry changes, skill development, and strategic decisions. | 定义概念 | It includes promotions, industry changes, skill development, and strategic decisions. |
| First you explain…; third, you describe key transitions or promotions. | 讲述结构 | Third, you describe key transitions or promotions. |
| Many professionals switch careers at some point. | 安抚转行顾虑 | Many professionals switch careers at some point. |
| That makes the switch sound logical, not random. | 评价转行说法 | That makes the switch sound logical, not random. |
| My long-term goal is to move into executive management. | 讲长期目标 | Or my long-term goal is to move into executive management. |
| Notice the transition words: after, where, eventually, and currently. | 提示时间连接词 | Notice the transition words after, where, after two years, eventually, and currently. |
| That explanation highlights motivation and preparation. | 评价回答质量 | That explanation highlights motivation and preparation. |
| Like moving from junior to senior positions. | 举例说明晋升 | Like moving from junior to senior positions. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Today, we're talking about how to talk about your career path in English. | 今天我们聊如何用英语谈自己的职业路径。 |
| I started my career in operations as a logistics coordinator. | 我的职业生涯从运营部物流专员开始。 |
| Where I gained hands-on experience in financial reporting. | 在那里我积累了财务报告的实操经验。 |
| After two years, I was promoted to marketing executive. | 两年后，我晋升为市场主管。 |
| Which allowed me to manage client relationships. | 这让我有机会管理客户关系。 |
| Eventually, I transitioned into digital marketing, specializing in data analytics. | 最终我转入了数字营销，专攻数据分析。 |
| After five years in finance, I decided to pivot into the technology sector. | 在金融行业干了五年后，我决定转向科技行业。 |
| The word pivot is useful in professional contexts. | pivot 这个词在职场语境里很实用。 |
| It includes promotions, industry changes, skill development, and strategic decisions. | 它包括晋升、行业转换、技能成长和战略性决定。 |
| Third, you describe key transitions or promotions. | 第三步，描述关键的转型或晋升。 |
| Many professionals switch careers at some point. | 很多职场人都会在某个阶段转行。 |
| That makes the switch sound logical, not random. | 这样转行听起来就有逻辑，而不是随性而为。 |
| That's important, especially if you switched industries. | 这很重要，尤其是当你换过行业时。 |
| My long-term goal is to move into executive management. | 我的长期目标是进入高管管理层。 |
| Notice the transition words after, where, after two years, eventually, and currently. | 注意这些衔接词：after、where、after two years、eventually、currently。 |
| That explanation highlights motivation and preparation. | 这个回答突出了动机和准备。 |
| Because it shows intention and motivation. | 因为它体现了意图和动力。 |
| Like moving from junior to senior positions. | 比如从初级岗位升到资深岗位。 |
