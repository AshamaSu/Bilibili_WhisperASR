| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| From saying good morning to asking for help, chatting during lunch, and even handling small talk. | 从道早安到求助、午餐闲聊，甚至应对小型寒暄。 |
| Sophia, do you have a minute? | Sophia，有空聊一分钟吗？ |
| Now let's talk about apologizing. | 现在我们来聊聊道歉。 |
| I'm having trouble understanding this data. | 我不太看得懂这份数据。 |
| Of course, let me see. | 当然，我看看。 |
| And on Friday you can say, have a great weekend! | 周五你可以说：周末愉快！ |
| It's at 10.30, right? | 是 10 点半，对吧？ |
| Any suggestions? | 有什么建议吗？ |
| Hey, Sophia, good morning, how's it going? | 嗨 Sophia，早上好，怎么样呀？ |
| That's okay. These things happen. | 没关系，这种事难免。 |
| Good morning, Daniel. I'm doing pretty well, thanks. I feel quite productive today. | 早上好 Daniel，我挺好的，谢谢。今天效率挺高。 |
| I see your point, but we also need to meet the client's expectations. | 我明白你的意思，但我们也得满足客户的期望。 |
| I'd love to, but I have a meeting. | 我很想去，但我有个会。 |
| Sophia, can I ask your opinion on something? | Sophia，我能听听你对一件事的看法吗？ |
| Do you want to grab lunch or are you free for coffee later? | 想一起吃午饭吗？或者晚点喝杯咖啡？ |
| …we usually move into small talk. | ……我们通常会先闲聊几句。 |
| Sounds good. | 听起来不错。 |
| All right, everyone. | 好了，各位。 |
| We're here to help you speak English naturally. | 我们在这里帮你自然地说英语。 |
| And practice every day. | 并且每天练习。 |
