## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Instead of saying, I want more money, you can say, I'd like to discuss my compensation. | 别说「我想要更多钱」，可以说「我想聊聊我的报酬」。 |
| What's the salary range for this position? | 这个职位的薪资区间是多少？ |
| I'd like to review my compensation based on my recent contributions. | 我想基于近期的贡献，重新评估一下我的报酬。 |
| Could there be flexibility in the salary range? | 薪资区间上还有商量的余地吗？ |
| Benefits are things you receive in addition to your salary. | 福利是工资之外你还能拿到的东西。 |
| Health insurance, paid vacation, bonuses, retirement plans, these are all benefits. | 医保、带薪年假、奖金、养老金，这些都算福利。 |
| Benefits can include health insurance, vacation days, bonuses, flexible working hours. | 福利可以包括医保、年假、奖金、弹性工作时间。 |
| Don't forget about perks or benefits like flexible working hours, remote options. | 别忘了弹性工时、远程办公这些额外待遇。 |
| A common mistake is starting with emotion, saying, I feel underpaid. | 常见错误是一开口就带情绪：「我觉得我工资太低」。 |
| This language sounds indirect, but it's actually very powerful. | 这种说法听着委婉，其实非常有力量。 |
| Salary conversations are stressful for everyone, even native speakers. | 谈薪对谁都很有压力，母语者也不例外。 |
| That shows appreciation while opening the door for negotiation. | 这既表达了感谢，又为谈判留了口子。 |
| You're negotiating a job offer. | 你在谈一份工作 offer。 |
| If you're paid by the hour, you earn wages. | 如果按小时计酬，那你挣的是时薪（wages）。 |
| Wages are hourly. | wages 是按小时算的。 |
| Your income might include your salary, bonuses, freelance work, or investments. | 收入可能包括工资、奖金、副业或投资收益。 |
| Mistake number two, saying, I want more money during a review. | 错误二：在绩效面谈时直接说「我想要更多钱」。 |
| Is it interviews, asking for a raise, or talking with coworkers? | 是面试、申请加薪，还是和同事聊？ |
