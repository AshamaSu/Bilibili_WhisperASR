## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Good morning and welcome to our office. It's great to finally meet you in person. | 早上好，欢迎来到我们办公室。终于见到你本人了。 |
| How was your trip? | 一路还顺利吗？ |
| Can I get you some coffee, tea or water before we start? | 开始前要来点咖啡、茶还是水？ |
| We have allocated funding, but we want to ensure strong return on investment. | 预算我们已经批了，但希望确保投资回报够高。 |
| One concern I have is disruption. Our teams are already under pressure. | 我的一个顾虑是业务中断，我们的团队本来压力就大。 |
| We provide onboarding sessions and ongoing support. | 我们提供上手培训和持续支持。 |
| We often include workshops to address concerns early. | 我们通常会安排工作坊，尽早化解顾虑。 |
| Change management will be essential then. | 那么变革管理就非常关键了。 |
| Before we wrap up, do you have any concerns or questions we haven't covered yet? | 结束之前，还有没有我们没聊到的顾虑或问题？ |
| Our pricing is typically based on scope rather than a fixed package. | 我们的报价通常按范围来，而不是固定套餐。 |
| We can prepare a detailed proposal with cost breakdowns and projected ROI. | 我们可以准备一份详细提案，含费用明细和预期投资回报。 |
| The full project timeline would depend on the scope. | 完整的项目工期取决于范围。 |
| Our system complies with international security standards and we conduct regular audits. | 我们的系统符合国际安全标准，并定期审计。 |
| Short weekly updates plus monthly strategic reviews. | 简短的周报加月度战略回顾。 |
| In the early stages, we would need input from key stakeholders. | 前期我们需要主要相关方的意见。 |
| That's important to us. | 这对我们很重要。 |
| That's good to know. | 这个信息很有用。 |
| Efficiency and communication are two of your main concerns. | 效率和沟通是你们最关心的两件事。 |
