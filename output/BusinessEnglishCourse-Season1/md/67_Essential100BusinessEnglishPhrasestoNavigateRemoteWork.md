## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Welcome back to the Deep Dive, the place where we really try to cut through all the noise, dive deep into the information ocean, and pull out the exact language you need. | 欢迎回到 Deep Dive，我们在这里剥开噪音、扎进信息海洋，把你要的那句原话捞出来。 |
| It pushes everyone to think about what really needs live discussion versus what could be an email. | 它逼着大家想清楚：什么真需要开会，什么发封邮件就行。 |
| Seeing faces definitely boosts engagement, helps read the room, reduces misunderstanding. | 看见人脸确实能提升参与度、帮你判断气氛、减少误解。 |
| Let's find overlap between New York and London hours so nobody must join before dawn. | 我们找一段纽约和伦敦时间的重叠，别让谁天没亮就得开会。 |
| Making the abstract concept of time zones visual makes finding fair slots much easier. | 把抽象的时区可视化，找公平时段就容易多了。 |
| You need a pre-agreed fallback, a standard protocol everyone knows. | 你需要一个事先约定、大家都知道的备用方案。 |
| If urgent but non-agenda things pop up mid-call, the protocol is to just type parking lot in the chat. | 如果会中冒出紧急但不在议程的事，规矩是在聊天框打 parking lot。 |
| Something like, can we allocate five minutes at the end for action items, confirming who owns each follow-up task? | 比如：最后留五分钟过行动项，确认每个后续任务归谁。 |
| You move from a vague next step to a clearly owned action item. | 这就把模糊的“下一步”变成有人负责的行动项。 |
| Granular accountability right away makes big tasks feel less daunting. | 一开始就把责任拆细，大任务就不那么吓人。 |
| Sustained overwork kills productivity and leads to burnout, period. | 长期超时工作会毁掉效率、导致倦怠，就这样。 |
| In a remote world, you just can't rely on osmosis, you know, or casual proximity or physical presence to get expectations across. | 在远程环境里，你不能指望别人“自然领会”，也不能靠挨着坐、靠人在场来传达预期。 |
| The simple phrase, use inclusive language like team instead of guys, fostering belonging in distributed environments. | 一句话：用 team 这类包容性称呼代替 guys，在分布式团队里培养归属感。 |
| If bandwidth drops suddenly, please switch off video first. | 如果带宽突然变差，请先关掉视频。 |
| It focuses directly on respecting sleep cycles, well-being. | 它直接关注尊重睡眠周期和身心状态。 |
