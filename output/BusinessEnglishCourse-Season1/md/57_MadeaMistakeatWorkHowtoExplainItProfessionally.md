## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Today, we're really getting into the weeds of high-stakes professional conversations. | 今天我们真的进入高风险职场对话的细节里。 |
| First up is phase one, the language of immediate accountability, basically owning the miss right away. | 第一步是“立刻担责”的说法，也就是第一时间认下这个失误。 |
| That's taking ownership instantly. | 这就是瞬间把责任担起来。 |
| Then they move right into analyzing the cause of the slip up, crucially, not the character of the person who slipped up. | 然后他们直接分析失误的原因——关键是，不是分析出错那个人的人品。 |
| It frames it as a systemic question, not just an individual failing. | 它把问题框成系统层面的问题，而不只是某个人的失手。 |
| Phase three is about building the fix, designing technical and process safeguards so it doesn't happen again. | 第三步是建立修复机制，设计技术和流程上的防护，让它不再发生。 |
| And finally, phase four, cultivating a learning culture, making sure the lessons actually stick. | 最后是第四步，培养复盘文化，让教训真正留下来。 |
| In a crisis, corporate jargon or overly careful legalistic phrasing can sound like you're hiding something or deflecting. | 危机时刻，公司套话或过度谨慎的法律式措辞，听起来就像你在隐瞒或推责。 |
| If you want people to be honest next time, you reward that transparency, even when the news is bad. | 如果你希望下次大家还说真话，就要奖励这种坦白，哪怕消息很糟。 |
| The manager asks, what downstream impact are we facing and who needs a heads up before noon? | 主管会问：我们要面对哪些下游影响，中午前要通知谁？ |
| It signals true leadership maturity. | 这体现了真正的领导成熟度。 |
| To ensure the analysis is objective, systematic, not emotionally charged or rushed, the focus is dissecting the process, not the person. | 为了保证分析客观、系统、不带情绪也不仓促，焦点是拆流程，而不是拆人。 |
| That timing before noon, it forces an immediate focus on consequences, resource allocation, communication urgency. | 中午这个时限，会逼着你立刻关注后果、资源调配和沟通的紧迫性。 |
| The person admitted, I skipped the secondary review because the client pinged me repeatedly for confirmation. | 那个人承认：客户反复催我确认，我就跳过了二次复核。 |
| Pressure happens, yet bypassing safeguards exposes us. | 压力总会存在，但绕过防护措施就会让我们暴露在风险里。 |
| It sort of sets a boundary for the next time without sounding punitive. | 它相当于给下次划了条边界，又不显得像在罚人。 |
| They ask the employee to identify their own signals of being overloaded. | 他们让员工自己说出超负荷的信号。 |
| And unfortunately, the variance traces back to my approval. | 很遗憾，这个偏差可以追溯到我当时的审批。 |
