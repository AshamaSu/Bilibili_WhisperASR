## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| It's the difference between sounding perfectly correct and sounding completely natural. | 差别就在于：说得完全正确，和说得完全自然。 |
| You know the grammar, your vocabulary list is massive, yet when you talk to native speakers, you still feel like you're reading from a formal script. | 语法你都懂、词汇量也大，可一和母语者说话，还是觉得自己在念稿子。 |
| Well, that initial greeting, it's a low stakes moment, but it can have a really high impact. | 开头那句问候看着不重要，影响却可能很大。 |
| Pretty good, thanks is arguably the most common and natural sounding positive answer you'll hear. | “挺好，谢谢”大概是你最常听到、也最自然的正面回答。 |
| Interestingly, pretty good in this context usually means very good. | 有意思的是，这里说的“挺好”通常就是“很好”。 |
| Or for a more casual, cool closure with familiar colleagues, you often hear, catch you later. | 和熟人更随意地收尾，常听到的就是“回头见”。 |
| The perfect soft start is almost always something like, I see your point, but... Start with validation. | 最好的软开场几乎都是“我明白你的意思，不过……”——先认可对方。 |
| Saying, I get where you're coming from, shows you understand the experiences, the feelings, maybe the history that led them to their opinion. | 说“我理解你的处境”，表明你懂对方的经历、感受，也许还有让他形成这个看法的来龙去脉。 |
| Plugging along is a great phrase for signaling steady, reliable, consistent effort. | “还在慢慢推进”很适合表达稳定、靠谱、持续的努力。 |
| It just brings you right back together, instantly validating the relationship despite however much time has passed. | 它让你们立刻重新靠近，不管隔了多久，都瞬间确认了这层关系。 |
| And then there's the quiet sort of self-possessed confidence of can't complain. | 还有一种安静的、笃定的自信，就是“没啥可抱怨的”。 |
| But crucially, it still maintains this humble, approachable tone. | 但关键是，它依然保持着谦虚、好接近的语气。 |
| Our goal for you, the listener, is to maybe identify which of these keys feel right for your personality and your professional context. | 我们的目标是让你找出，哪几把钥匙更贴合你的性格和职场场景。 |
| They show you are actually paying attention to the shared context. | 它们表明你确实在关注你们共处的语境。 |
| It's more a gentle acknowledgement of their presence. | 它更像是对对方在场的一种温和回应。 |
| If that initial greeting feels stiff, the whole conversation kind of risks freezing up, doesn't it? | 如果开场问候太僵，整段对话就容易冷场，对吧？ |
