## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Look, if you're trying to really get a handle on business English, I mean, the real practical stuff people actually say day to day. | 如果你真想把商务英语拿下来，我说的是大家日常真会说的那套。 |
| It shows how important it is not just to identify a problem, but to know and follow the process for dealing with it. | 这说明不只是要发现问题，还得知道并走完处理流程。 |
| Have you followed the established protocol? | 你走过既定规程了吗？ |
| Okay, you need to log a ticket. | 好，你需要提一个工单。 |
| And because it seems systemic, the next step is escalation. | 因为看起来是系统性问题，下一步就是上报升级。 |
| It immediately sets up this cooperative framework. | 它立刻搭起一个协作的框架。 |
| The reply, good idea, I'll do that, confirms receipt and intention to comply. | “好主意，我来办”这句回复，确认了收到和执行意愿。 |
| That praise first, then specific and actionable critique structure is incredibly effective. | 先肯定、再给具体可执行建议，这种反馈结构极其有效。 |
| Committing immediately to the next tangible action, setting up the meeting, shows you're serious and builds momentum. | 立刻承诺下一个具体动作（比如把会排上），既显诚意又能推动进程。 |
| These conversations are often about reducing uncertainty or ensuring compliance. | 这类对话往往是为了减少不确定性或确保合规。 |
| What's our policy on remote work? | 我们远程办公的政策是什么？ |
| We're looking at the broader context now, the actual framework of the workplace, what we're terming workplace governance. | 现在看更大的背景，也就是工作场所的实际框架，我们称之为职场治理。 |
| We also saw mentions of longer term comfort things like ergonomic chairs being ordered. | 我们也看到一些长期舒适度方面的提法，比如订购人体工学椅。 |
| Work-life balance, remote work policies. | 工作与生活的平衡、远程办公政策。 |
| Please make sure to back up your files first. | 请务必先备份文件。 |
| Let me take a look. | 我看一下。 |
