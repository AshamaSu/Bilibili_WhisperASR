## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you have a moment to talk? | 约老板谈话（开场） | Good morning, Sarah. Do you have a moment to talk? |
| What's on your mind? | 老板开场问事由 | Yes, I have a few minutes. What's on your mind? |
| I wanted to talk about my recent tasks and check if I'm on the right track. | 汇报并确认方向 | I wanted to talk about my recent tasks and check if I'm on the right track. |
| Could you explain what you mean? | 请求澄清反馈 | One thing you can improve is the introduction. Could you explain what you mean? |
| Would it be possible to move the deadline to Thursday? | 协商顺延截止日期 | Would it be possible to submit the report on Monday instead? |
| I'm not sure if I'm meeting expectations. | 主动问表现 | Sometimes I'm not sure if I'm meeting expectations. |
| One thing you could work on is speaking up more in meetings. | 上级给反馈 | One thing you could work on is speaking up more in meetings. |
| Can I add something here? | 会议礼貌插话 | You can say something like, can I add something here? |
| Sorry, could you say that again? | 没听清请对方重复 | You can say, sorry, could you say that again? |
| I'd like to grow in the company and take on more responsibility. | 谈职业发展 | I'd like to grow in the company and take on more responsibility. |
| What would you recommend I focus on next? | 请教提升方向 | What would you recommend I focus on next? |
| Asking for help shows awareness and professionalism. | 求助不丢人 | Actually, it shows awareness and professionalism. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Do you have a moment to talk? | 你现在有空聊几句吗？ |
| What's on your mind? | 你想聊什么？ |
| I wanted to talk about my recent tasks and check if I'm on the right track. | 我想聊聊手头的任务，确认一下方向对不对。 |
| Could you explain what you mean? | 你能具体说说是什么意思吗？ |
| Would it be possible to move the deadline to Thursday? | 截止日期能不能挪到周四？ |
| I'm not sure if I'm meeting expectations. | 我不确定自己是否达到了预期。 |
| One thing you could work on is speaking up more in meetings. | 你可以改进的一点，是在会上多发个言。 |
| Can I add something here? | 这里我能补充一句吗？ |
| Sorry, could you say that again? | 不好意思，能再说一遍吗？ |
| I'd like to grow in the company and take on more responsibility. | 我想在公司成长，承担更多职责。 |
| What would you recommend I focus on next? | 您建议我接下来重点提升什么？ |
| Asking for help shows awareness and professionalism. | 求助恰恰体现了自知和专业。 |
| What matters is clarity, not perfection. | 重要的是清晰，而不是完美。 |
| It's better to do fewer things well. | 少做几件事、把它们做好，反而更好。 |
| Senior managers usually want the main message quickly. | 高层通常想最快听到核心结论。 |
| Good communication is always better than silence. | 主动沟通永远好过沉默。 |
| Confidence comes from practice, not perfection. | 自信来自练习，而不是完美。 |
| My door is always open. | 我的门随时为你敞开（有问题随时来）。 |
