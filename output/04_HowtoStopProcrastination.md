## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Yes, today's topic is how to beat procrastination at work. | 是的，今天的主题是如何战胜职场拖延。 |
| Many professionals have important tasks to complete, but sometimes they delay starting. | 许多职场人有重要任务要完成，但有时迟迟不肯开始。 |
| I noticed that some of your recent tasks were delayed. | 我注意到你最近的一些任务延期了。 |
| I don't know where to start, so I end up doing smaller, less important tasks instead. | 我不知道从哪儿下手，结果净去做些更小、更不重要的琐事。 |
| I want the report to be perfect so I keep delaying it. | 我想把报告做到完美，于是一直拖着。 |
| That's a classic sign of procrastination. You're busy, but not productive. | 那是拖延的典型信号：你很忙，但没产出。 |
| That does sound like a large task. Maybe we can divide it into smaller steps. | 这听起来确实是个大任务。也许我们可以把它拆成更小的步骤。 |
| So instead of thinking about the entire report, you can focus on just one step at a time. | 所以别总想着整份报告，一次只专注一个步骤就好。 |

| So just start even if it's not perfect. | 所以先动手做，哪怕不完美。 |

| I would also schedule regular check-ins to track progress. | 我还会安排定期的进度对齐来跟踪进展。 |
| Perfectionism often leads to procrastination. | 完美主义常常导致拖延。 |
