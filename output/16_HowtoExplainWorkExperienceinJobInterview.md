## 2. 句式

| 句式 | 使用场景 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you tell me about your work experience? | 面试经典提问 | Can you tell me about your work experience? |
| I have over five years of experience in marketing. | 报年限开场 | I have over five years of experience in marketing. |
| I started my career as a sales assistant. | 讲职业起点 | I started my career as a sales assistant, where I learned how to communicate with customers. |
| Then I moved on to a marketing role. | 讲进阶转折 | Then I moved on to a marketing role where I managed social media campaigns. |
| Currently, I work as a sales manager. | 讲现状 | Currently, I work as a sales manager, leading a team of five. |
| I was responsible for managing customer inquiries. | 讲职责 | I was responsible for managing customer inquiries and handling complaints. |
| One of my key achievements was increasing sales by 20%. | 讲成果 | One of my key achievements was increasing sales by 20% in one year. |
| I increased sales by 15% in six months. | 数字化成果 | I increased sales by 15% in six months. |
| Duties are what you were supposed to do. | 辨析 duty/achievement | Duties are what you were supposed to do. Achievements are what you did successfully. |
| Use past tense for previous jobs. | 时态建议 | Use past tense for previous jobs and present tense for your current role. |
| Instead of saying…, you should say… | 弱句改强句 | Instead of saying, I answered customer emails, say, I handled customer inquiries and improved response time by 30%. |
| I believe my experience has prepared me well for this position. | 收尾自信句 | I believe my experience has prepared me well for this position. |

## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Today we're going to learn how to explain your work experience in a job interview. | 今天我们要学的是如何在面试中介绍你的工作经验。 |
| Can you tell me about your work experience? | 能介绍一下你的工作经验吗？ |
| Many people make the mistake of giving either too little information or too much. | 很多人的错误是要么说太少，要么说太多。 |
| You can break it into three parts: past, present, and key achievements. | 你可以分成三部分：过去、现在和关键成就。 |
| I started my career as a sales assistant where I learned how to communicate with customers. | 我从销售助理做起，在那里学会了如何与客户沟通。 |
| After that, I moved into a sales executive role where I managed client accounts. | 之后我升到销售主管，负责管理客户账户。 |
| Currently, I work as a sales manager, leading a team of five. | 目前我担任销售经理，带一个五人团队。 |
| I was responsible for managing customer inquiries and handling complaints. | 我负责处理客户咨询和投诉。 |
| Duties are what you were supposed to do. Achievements are what you did successfully. | 职责是你分内该做的，成就是你成功做出来的。 |
| Numbers make your experience more powerful. | 数字会让你的经验更有说服力。 |
| I increased sales by 15% in six months. | 我在六个月内把销售额提高了 15%。 |
| STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, and Result. | STAR 分别指情境、任务、行动和结果。 |
| As a result, customer satisfaction increased by 25%. | 结果客户满意度提升了 25%。 |
| You can talk about internships, projects, or volunteering. | 你可以讲实习、项目或志愿经历。 |
| Any relevant experience counts. | 任何相关经历都算数。 |
| Mistake number one, being too vague. | 第一个错误：说得太笼统。 |
| Use past tense for previous jobs and present tense for your current role. | 过去的工作用过去时，现在的岗位用现在时。 |
| Clarity is more important than speed. | 清晰比语速更重要。 |
| One of my key achievements was increasing our online engagement by 30%. | 我的一项关键成就是让线上互动量提升了 30%。 |
| I believe my experience has prepared me well for this position. | 我相信我的经验已让我充分胜任这个职位。 |
