## 3. 词句翻译

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| How to talk about salary negotiation in business English. | 如何用商务英语谈薪资。 |
| At some point, the interviewer may ask, what are your salary expectations? | 面试进行到某个节点，面试官可能会问：你的薪资期望是多少？ |
| Now let's talk about an important strategy, giving a range instead of a single number. | 我们来聊一个重要策略：报一个区间，而不是一个单独数字。 |
| Many people feel nervous when talking about salary. | 很多人一谈薪资就紧张。 |
| Mistake number one, not doing research. | 第一个错误：不做调研。 |
| You want to know the average for your role, your experience, and your location. | 你要了解你的岗位、经验所在地对应的平均水平。 |
| I want a bigger salary or I need more money. | （不要这样说：）我想要更多工资/我需要更多钱。 |
| Exactly, you're not rejecting, you're negotiating. | 没错——你不是在拒绝，而是在谈判。 |
| Now let's talk about negotiating benefits. | 现在我们聊聊福利谈判。 |
| Thank you for the offer. | 感谢贵司的录用通知。 |
| Congratulations. We'd like to offer you the position. | 恭喜！我们想录用你担任该职位。 |
| The company gives you an offer, but the salary is lower than expected. | 公司给了 offer，但薪资低于预期。 |
| You are discussing your value. | 你是在讨论你的价值。 |
| What if you don't want to give a number first? | 如果你不想先报数字怎么办？ |
| …could we discuss additional benefits? | ……我们能聊聊额外的福利吗？ |
| Mistake two, being too aggressive. | 第二个错误：姿态太过强硬。 |
| For example, you can negotiate flexible hours, remote work, or extra vacation days. | 比如，你可以谈弹性工时、远程办公或额外的年假。 |
| I understand. | 我理解。 |
| What happens next? | 接下来怎么走？ |
| …please give us a thumbs up and don't forget to subscribe. | ……请给我们点个赞，别忘了订阅。 |
| Your support means everything to us. | 你们的支持对我们意义重大。 |
